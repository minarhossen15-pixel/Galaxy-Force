Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

Galaxy Force ("we", "our", or "us") operates this client-side, offline-first space shooter game. We prioritize user privacy and hold transparency as a core design principle.

Transparency Guarantee: All gameplay upgrades, progression levels, and setting layouts are processed and saved strictly on your local device hardware.

1. No Personal Data Collection

Because the game is built using local web view sandboxed architectures, we do not require user registrations or profile creations. We do not collect, intercept, or share personal identifying information such as names, geolocation logs, phone numbers, or email addresses.

2. Local Client-Side Persistence

Your game levels, gold coins, gems, ship stats, custom upgrades, and preferences remain isolated on your device's offline web container storage (localStorage). It is completely secure and cannot be accessed by external database nodes.

3. Transparency & Simulated Systems

Our ad mechanics and backup setups operate entirely client-side using native UI simulations. They are integrated only to serve as entertaining offline gameplay loops and do NOT transmit or leak telemetry or browser profiling details to ad networks.

4. Complete Data Control & Deletion

You have absolute control over your digital footprint. If you ever wish to erase your progress, settings, and unlock history, simply launch the in-game Settings menu and click on the "Wipe All Progress" button. This immediately flushes your local storage container.
