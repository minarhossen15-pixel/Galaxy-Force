Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Galaxy Force ("we", "our", or "us") operates this client-side space shooter game. Our application is offline-first, run directly in the client browser/webview, and prioritizes user privacy.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect, store, or share any personal identity information. All game progression, ship upgrades, settings preferences, and score statistics are stored locally on your device via client-side storage (localStorage).

2. Third-Party Services

This game operates independently on local web technologies. It does not integrate external tracking SDKs, unauthorized telemetry tools, or background analysis services without user consent.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

Your gameplay data remains entirely on your physical device. You can fully delete or wipe all progress and local settings at any time using the "Wipe All Progress" option in the game's Settings menu.
